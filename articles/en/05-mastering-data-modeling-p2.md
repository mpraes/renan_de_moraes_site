This practical guide explores enterprise best practices for building robust, scalable, and maintainable data models.

### Key Principles:
- **Standardized Naming Conventions**: Enforcing consistent naming across tables, keys, and metrics.
- **Null Handling**: Strategies for avoiding aggregation anomalies.
- **Fact and Dimension Clarity**: Defining granular levels of detail.
- **Data Dictionary Documentation**: Aligning technical specifications with business domain terminology.
