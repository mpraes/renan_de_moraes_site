## Overview
**Lumenex (Lumen)** is an interactive web product engineered for guided biblical exegesis focused on New Testament textual research. It implements the methodological principles of Eric Lund’s hermeneutical rules combined with Gordon Fee’s operational exegetical workflow framework.

## Key Features
- **Guided Hermeneutical Engine**: Interactive step-by-step exegetical workflow applying Lund's rules and Gordon Fee's textual analysis methods.
- **Original Language & Morphological Lookup**: Integrated tools for original language verse inspection, word study, and contextual analysis.
- **Structured Research Notes**: System for recording textual commentary, historical context, and structural breakdown per passage.
- **Cloud Web Access**: Available online at [www.lumenex.com.br](https://www.lumenex.com.br).
