## Overview
**Verometric** is a proprietary SaaS product designed for applied data intelligence and automated business analytics. It transforms complex enterprise metrics into actionable executive dashboards, automated anomaly alerts, and decision-support insights.

## Key Features
- **Applied Data Intelligence**: Automates calculation of key operational metrics, revenue KPIs, and growth trends.
- **Automated Anomaly Detection**: Monitors data streams for statistical anomalies and delivers proactive alerts.
- **Interactive Dashboards**: High-performance UI rendering real-time business telemetry and custom data visualization.
- **Enterprise-Grade Security**: Built with role-based access control (RBAC), multi-tenant data isolation, and encrypted data transport.
