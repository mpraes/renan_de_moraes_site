Database normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF) is vital for ensuring relational integrity and eliminating anomalies in OLTP transactional systems.

### Core Concepts:

- **1NF**: Eliminating repeating groups and enforcing atomic values.
- **2NF**: Removing partial dependencies on composite primary keys.
- **3NF**: Eliminating transitive dependencies.
- **Denormalization (OLAP)**: In Data Warehousing, intentional denormalization into Star Schemas optimizes complex analytical query performance.
